The Relationship Between Income and Expenses

Ideally, entrepreneurs want their income to be more than expenses. Sometimes, this might not be the case and they will need to borrow money to cover the difference. However, this strategy is not sustainable for the long term.

One way to avoid going into debt to cover expenses is by creating a budget. A budget is a way to keep track of planned expenses to ensure there is enough income to cover them, and reach savings goals.

If budgeted expenses are more than budgeted income, then entrepreneurs must find ways to cut expenses. One way to do this is to focus on essential expenses (needs) and get rid of nonessential expenses (wants).
